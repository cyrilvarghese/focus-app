Isometric studio: one room, four pals

This is a throwaway prototype of the scene only. It's one inline SVG drawn from grid coordinates, and every animation is a CSS loop, so no JavaScript runs per frame.

  • Working: heads nod slowly, arms rock as they shape, the turntables spin (the lines slide around each piece), and mint "focus" sparks flicker now and then.
  • Away: the pal fades to grey, their head droops, the z's float up, and their hands and wheel stop. Nothing on the table is undone.
  • Ambient: the plant in the corner sways, steam rises from the shared mug, and the window light slowly brightens and dims.
  • Everything stops under reduced motion.

Two pals sit on each back edge of one table, so they're across from each other rather than in a row. The shelf on the wall holds finished pieces (your collection), and the shared things sit in the middle of the table.
